Privacy policy

Outreach Desk by MaximusLabs · last updated 20 November 2026

What this site is

Outreach Desk is an internal workspace that MaximusLabs uses to run link-exchange outreach for its clients, and to show each client an overview of that outreach. It is not open to the public: only people whose email address MaximusLabs has added can see any data.

What we receive when you sign in with Google

We ask Google only for your name, email address and profile picture, to confirm who you are. We do not ask for, read or store your Gmail, contacts, files or any other Google data.

How we use it

  • To check whether your email address has been given access, and to which client's overview.
  • To record which signed-in team member took an action on the desk (for example sending a reply).

We do not sell, share or use this information for advertising.

Where it is stored

Sign-in details and desk data are stored with our database provider, Supabase. Access is limited by the email addresses MaximusLabs has approved.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time. To have your access or sign-in record removed, email partners@maximuslabs.ai.
